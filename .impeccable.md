## Design Context

### Users
Primary users are UC Davis students living near campus who are helping RoamingOS with early commercial research. They need to describe a week of planned movement quickly, often from a laptop or phone, without typing full addresses.

### Brand Personality
The interface should feel trustworthy, clear, and lightly futuristic. It should reduce friction, feel organized and research-grade, and make students comfortable sharing planned movement patterns.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use a light-mode-first editorial operations aesthetic with warm paper neutrals, grounded greens, and restrained signal accents. The experience should feel more like a thoughtful campus logistics studio than a generic startup dashboard. Avoid dark neon UI, loud gradient-heavy visuals, and over-gamified survey patterns.

### Design Principles
- Replace address entry with numbered destinations and simple route notation.
- Keep the weekly planner readable before making it visually clever.
- Explain what data is being collected and why in plain language.
- Make the same workflow work smoothly on both desktop and mobile.
- Show pricing logic as an early research preview, not a final product promise.
