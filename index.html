The Pain Point

No one stands for the car owner in this business.

Ride-hailing market chain:
Demand→ Dispatch→ Match→ Operate→ Supply

Whoever controls the match controls who wins.

Matching— deciding which trips you accept and which you decline

It is car owners' right!

You can only maximize your benefit if you defend it.

Uber will not kindly do you a favor

It can only be worse if you give the matching right to them

evidence

By one analysis, Uber's take rate is now ~42% (up from 32% before “upfront pricing”) and runs as high as 65–70% on individual rides — a number only the platform can confirm. — National Employment Law Project, 2025

The Solution

Representing AV owners in every interaction with ride-hailing platforms.

Loyalty is to the owner — protecting the owner's access first, then maximizing the value of every idle hour.
The Engine

Kallos

A selective memory of when each owner needs their car, read out as a calibrated boundary: how far it may roam, and when it must return.

Calibrated access decision

Is this even possible?
People are far more regular than they feel — but prediction only works when the system has enough signal.

A landmark Science study measured ~93% potential predictability in human movement; a 500,000-person follow-up found ~88%. People are far more regular than they feel. But that ceiling holds in data-rich conditions. — Song et al., Science; Lu et al., Scientific Reports

Market

The second wave is larger by an order of magnitude or two.

Commercial robotaxis are the first wave — capital-expenditure-bound, expanded one vehicle at a time. Privately-owned AVs are the second wave, with a ceiling one to two orders of magnitude larger, because they activate an already-existing, self-financing installed base of roughly 300 million chassis rather than a fleet that must be built car by car. Once technology and regulation cross threshold, the private-ownership S-curve will be far steeper than the commercial one.

Access-guaranteed earning
The owner declares when they need the car. We send it out to earn only within limits that still guarantee it returns in time — a constrained optimization the owner can trust from day one. No prediction required yet; every trip comes back as data on how that owner actually lives.
